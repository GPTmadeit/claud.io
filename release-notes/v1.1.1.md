## Claud.io 1.1.1, the big engine update

(The `v1.1.0` tag points at an older commit by mistake and has no release. 1.1.1 is the first release with these changes.)

Claud.io is a desktop creative studio by **ANRCY**: design, video, audio-reactive visuals and 3D in one app.

### Downloads

| File | What it is | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| `Claud.io-Setup-1.1.1.exe` | Installer (recommended) | Run it and follow the steps. Adds Start menu and desktop shortcuts, opens `.studio` files, updates itself. |
| `Claud.io-1.1.1-Portable.exe` | Portable app | Double-click to run, nothing is installed. Does not update itself. |
| `latest.yml`, `*.blockmap` | Update feed | Used by the installed app to update itself. You don't need to download these. |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | Checksums | Optional: compare with `Get-FileHash <file>` in PowerShell. |

**Already on 1.0.1?** The app updates itself: wait for **Update ready** in the top bar, or click the logo → **Check for updates**, then restart.

**SmartScreen:** the exe is not code-signed yet. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

### What's new

**Hardware acceleration**
- The GPU is used for 3D, shaders, 2D canvas drawing and video decode, including GPUs on Chromium's blocklist. Laptops use the fast GPU.
- Video export uses the hardware encoder when one is available, with a software fallback.
- The About window (click the logo) shows whether acceleration is on and which GPU is in use, with an on/off switch. If the graphics driver keeps crashing, Claud.io offers to restart without it.

**Audio**
- Six new reactive sources: sub-bass, low mids, presence, transients, stereo width and overall energy.
- The modulation matrix is much deeper. Each connection has offset, minimum/maximum, threshold, curve (linear, soft, strong, S-curve, switch), attack, release, invert and mute.
- Seven new targets: saturation, visualizer intensity, glow, and shader intensity, zoom, detail and distortion.
- Modulation profiles: save, load, replace, duplicate and delete them, or copy a set of modulations from one clip to another. Eight new built-in profiles.
- Live input: let visuals react to a microphone or line-in. It asks for permission first, shows a level meter and gain, records nothing, and exports always use the track.

**Visualizers, shaders and effects**
- Ten new visualizers: frequency spiral, interference, topography (real contour lines), fractal tree, kinetic field, 3D landscape, stereo Lissajous, orbitals, 3D tunnel and particle sphere.
- Twenty new visualizer presets in ten styles (minimal, geometric, organic, industrial, psychedelic, atmospheric, futuristic, tribal, abstract, spatial) and four new full looks.
- Six new shaders: domain warp, gyroid 3D (ray-marched), contour lines, reaction, Voronoi edges and silk. Every shader gains distortion, contrast and saturation controls.
- Four new video effects: chromatic aberration, motion blur, light rays and 2D distortion.

**3D studio**
- Up to six independent orbit systems per object, each with its own object, count, path, speed, tilt, material, audio source and visibility.
- New orbit paths: spiral, helix, figure eight, Lissajous and irregular, with complexity and deformation controls.
- Edit single companions: size, distance, offset, speed, height, or hide one.
- Camera moves: dolly, slow push-in, crane, orbit, sweep and handheld.

**Animation**
- Ten easing curves plus a custom Bezier curve, with easing set per keyframe if you want.
- Keyframes can loop or ping-pong, play in reverse, and start with an offset.
- Copy and paste keyframes between clips, or repeat them from the playhead.
- Nine new motion presets: elastic pop-in, pulse, gentle float, mechanical rotation, spiral, controlled chaos, rhythmic, slow cinematic and geometric growth.

**Interface**
- Workspaces: Design, Video, Audio & visualizer, 3D, Minimal and Advanced. Panels can be resized by dragging and the left side collapses with a double-click. Save, rename, duplicate, reset and delete your own layouts.
- New appearance options: density (compact, normal, spacious), interface size (85–130 %), high contrast, and four interface-motion levels (Full, Subtle, Reduced, Off). These never affect your designs, visualizers or videos.
- Appearance profiles: Dark Studio, Soft Night, High Contrast and Minimal, or save your own.
- The command palette (`Ctrl+K`) now finds every visualizer, shader, preset, workspace and appearance profile. Star a command to pin it to the top.
- Anrcy has four new expressions: success, confused, warning and explaining.
- Fixed: naming a set, a version or a brand kit failed in the desktop app (Electron has no `prompt()`). A small name dialog now opens instead.

### Compatibility

Projects, settings and saved sets from 1.0.x open unchanged. Old 3D objects keep their single orbit, and new options start switched off.
